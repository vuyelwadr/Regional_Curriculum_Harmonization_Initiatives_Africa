A Scoping Review of Regional Curriculum Harmonization

This interactive dashboard presents key findings from a comprehensive scoping review on curriculum harmonization in Africa. The review systematically analyzes 178 peer-reviewed articles and reports published between 2009 and 2025, revealing a dynamic and rapidly maturing field. Explore the data to understand the trends, focus areas, and future directions for educational policy across the continent.

Study Selection Process

The review followed the Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) scoping methodology. From an initial pool of 561 documents identified through programmatic searches, 178 studies were selected for final analysis based on pre-defined inclusion criteria.

Future Directions: Research Gaps & Priorities

This review identified significant gaps in the literature that point to clear priorities for future research. The tables below summarize key regional initiatives documented in the literature, the methodological approaches used, and the most critical areas needing further investigation to support evidence-informed policy.

Initiative/Framework Region Primary Focus Key Features / Approach
AU Harmonisation Strategy (HAQAA/PAQAF)ContinentalHigher Ed. Quality AssuranceContinental policy via Arusha/Addis Conventions, PAQAF standards, and HAQAA initiative.
EAC Protocol on Education and TrainingEast AfricaHigher Ed, TVET, HealthSystematic, policy-led alignment via its formal Protocol; most researched model.
ECOWAS Sector-Specific InitiativesWest AfricaProfessional Training (Health)Sector-based standardization; success in harmonizing health professional curricula.
SADC Protocol & Qualifications FrameworkSouthern AfricaHigher Ed. & TVETDecentralized, framework-guided model relying heavily on institutional collaboration.

Table 3: Priority Research Gaps

Research GapDescription of GapRecommended Action
Longitudinal Impact StudiesCritical lack of studies tracking initiatives over time to assess impact on mobility, employability, and institutional change.Conduct multi-year studies to measure long-term outcomes and ROI.
Stakeholder PerspectivesThe voices of students, academic staff, and employers are significantly underrepresented in the literature.Prioritize qualitative and survey-based research capturing grassroots perspectives.
Rigorous Comparative AnalysisFew studies systematically compare different regional models (e.g., EAC vs. SADC) to identify transferable lessons.Fund cross-regional comparative studies using standardized metrics.
Economic & Cost-Benefit AnalysisThe economic dimensions, including cost-benefit analyses of implementation, are virtually unexplored.Integrate economic modeling and cost-benefit analysis into evaluations.
Integrating DecolonizationLimited research on how frameworks can promote epistemic diversity and integrate African knowledge systems.Fund research on developing harmonization models that actively advance decolonization.
